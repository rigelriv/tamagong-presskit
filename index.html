About the game

Raise them your way.
Let your team do the fighting.

Make a home in Kernel Town and raise a team for the tournament. Your days are yours to spend: hatch eggs, train your monsters, meet the locals, or take a break by the sea.

Every monster has a branching skill tree. Pick a direction, combine abilities, and discover a team that works together. Three monsters fight at a time, with reserves ready to step in when one falls.
