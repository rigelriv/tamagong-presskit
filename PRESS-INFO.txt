TAMAGONG
Press and Creator Kit
Updated: 5 October 2026

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A monster-raising autobattler where you hatch, feed, and train your monsters,
shape their builds, and discover powerful team synergies.

ABOUT THE GAME
Raise your monsters in Kernel Town and prepare a team for the tournament.
Hatch eggs, feed and train your companions, and choose upgrades from their
branching skill trees. Bring six monsters into battle, field three at a time,
and choose a reserve when one of your active monsters falls.
